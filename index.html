PRACTICE WITH CONFIDENCE

Help with Raag Riyaz

Raag Riyaz is an offline-first Hindustani music practice companion for iPhone, iPad and Android. Your lessons, recordings and practice history stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Email support Read the privacy policy

Contact

For help, bug reports, accessibility feedback or privacy requests, email koushikp@alum.iisc.ac.in.

Please include the app version, device model, operating-system version and what happened. Do not send private recordings or teacher notes unless they are necessary and you are comfortable sharing them.

Microphone and recording

Open Settings on your device, find Raag Riyaz and confirm Microphone access is enabled. Reopen the app and make a short test recording after changing headphones or audio devices.

Bluetooth A2DP is playback-only. A Bluetooth microphone route may use lower-quality hands-free audio.

Notes, photos and PDFs

Write class notes directly in Classroom. Photos and files use system pickers. On Apple devices, scanning asks for camera access only when you choose Scan. On Android, Scan opens Google Play services’ document-scanner UI.

Swara exercises

Use a clear, comfortable voice in a quiet space. Select the correct SUR before starting. A short, steady touch on each swara is enough for Alankars and Vocal Challenges.

Back up important work

Use the app’s share or export controls to save important recordings and notes somewhere you control before deleting the app or changing devices.

Version 1.0 does not include an account or cross-device sync.

Delete local data

Delete individual recordings, lesson attachments and Library items using their in-app controls. Deleting Raag Riyaz removes its local app container. Apple backups may retain an older copy; Android backup and device transfer are disabled in version 1.0.

SUPPORTED PLATFORMS

Apple and Android

Supported platforms are iOS/iPadOS 16 or later and Android 8.0 or later. Native practice features do not require an account or subscription. Android’s optional document scanner may download a Google Play services component on first use.
