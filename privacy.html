PRIVACY POLICY

Your practice stays yours.

Raag Riyaz is developed and provided by Koushik PG. The current version is offline-first and does not require an account.

Effective 21 August 2026

Summary

Raag Riyaz does not contain advertising, developer-operated analytics or tracking, and it does not send your practice content to a developer-operated server. Practice settings, history, recordings, pitch observations, Classroom content and Raag Library content are processed and stored on your device. The Android document scanner’s limited Google Play services diagnostics are described below.

Microphone and audio

The app accesses the microphone only after you grant permission and start a swara exercise, Breath Test or recording feature. Pitch analysis runs on the device. Breath Test stores an improving duration result, not microphone audio. When you choose to record, the resulting audio is saved in the app’s private local storage. Microphone audio and pitch observations are not sent to us.

Camera, photos and files

Photos and files are selected through operating-system pickers or when you explicitly open a supported item in Raag Riyaz. On Apple devices, the app accesses the camera only when you choose to scan lesson notes. On Android, a Scan action opens Google Play services’ document-scanner UI; gallery import inside that scanner is disabled. Selected images, PDFs, scans, audio files, written notes and lesson metadata are copied into the app’s private Classroom or Raag Library storage.

Android document-scanner diagnostics

The Android app includes Google Play services ML Kit document scanner 16.0.0. Its scanner component may be downloaded on first use. Google states that ML Kit Android SDKs may collect device and application information, device or per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input/output size, feature version, feature-event types and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this metadata is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties. Scanned page content is processed on-device and is not included in the documented telemetry. This is Google Play services collection, not developer-operated Raag Riyaz analytics. See Google’s ML Kit Android data disclosure.

Practice history and settings

The app stores preferences, presets, session history, custom raags, learned-raaga state, coaching configuration and lesson dates locally. The current public interface does not offer MIDI setup. Conservative Bluetooth and local-network purpose strings remain in the binary for devices that retained a legacy beta MIDI configuration. If such a configuration is still enabled, MIDI messages are processed only on the device and practice content is not sent to us.

Sharing

Content leaves the app only when you explicitly use the device’s sharing or export controls, such as AirDrop, Files, Messages or another installed app. The destination you choose then handles the shared item under its own privacy terms. Raag Riyaz does not automatically upload your recordings or notes.

Apple external links

The Apple version may offer optional links to creator lessons on YouTube or the Raag Riyaaz fundraiser for Isha Vidhya on the Conscious Planet website. These pages open outside Raag Riyaz and are governed by their own privacy, cookie and payment policies. No payment details or donation status are returned to Raag Riyaz.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you voluntarily include. We use it only to respond, troubleshoot, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations—not for advertising. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes. You may request deletion at koushikp@alum.iisc.ac.in unless retention is legally required. Do not send private lesson content unless it is needed and you choose to do so.

Storage, backups and deletion

Version 1.0 has no developer-operated cloud sync. Delete individual items with their in-app controls. Deleting the app removes its local container. Apple device or iCloud backups may retain an older copy according to Apple’s settings and retention rules. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled, so clearing Android app data or uninstalling can permanently remove content that was not exported. Android Settings can create a complete versioned ZIP containing local text, settings, history and media; this version can share but cannot restore that ZIP.

Children

Raag Riyaz is a general-audience music practice tool and is not designated as a Kids Category app. The app has no account or developer-operated data-collection backend. A parent, guardian or teacher should supervise a child’s use of support email, device sharing and external links.

Security and changes

Your content benefits from the security controls of iOS, iPadOS or Android and the app’s private container, but no storage method is risk-free. We may update this policy when app behavior changes. The effective date above will change, and material changes will be disclosed through the app or its store listing when appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Koushik PG at koushikp@alum.iisc.ac.in.
