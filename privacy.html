PRIVACY POLICY

Your practice stays yours.

Raag Riyaz is developed and provided by Koushik PG. The current version is offline-first and does not require an account.

Effective 16 August 2026

Summary

Raag Riyaz does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs, track you across apps or websites, or send your practice content to a developer-operated server. Practice settings, history, recordings, pitch observations, Classroom content and Raag Library content are processed and stored on your device.

Microphone and audio

The app accesses the microphone only after you grant permission and start a swara exercise, Breath Test or recording feature. Pitch analysis runs on the device. Breath Test stores its result, not the microphone audio. When you choose to record, the resulting audio is saved in the app’s private local storage. Microphone audio and pitch observations are not sent to us.

Camera, photos and files

The app accesses the camera only when you choose to scan lesson notes. Photos and files are selected through Apple-provided pickers or when you explicitly open a supported item in Raag Riyaz. Selected images, PDFs, scans, audio files, written notes and lesson metadata are copied into the app’s private Classroom or Raag Library storage.

Practice history, settings and MIDI

The app stores preferences, presets, session history, custom raags, learned-raaga state, coaching configuration and lesson dates locally. MIDI messages, when enabled, are processed on the device and are not sent to us.

Sharing

Content leaves the app only when you explicitly use an Apple sharing feature, such as AirDrop, Files, Messages or another installed app. The destination you choose then handles the shared item under its own privacy terms. Raag Riyaz does not automatically upload your recordings or notes.

External lessons and fundraiser

Some optional links open creator lessons on YouTube or the Raag Riyaaz fundraiser for Isha Vidhya on the Conscious Planet website. These pages open outside Raag Riyaz and are governed by their own privacy, cookie and payment policies. Raag Riyaz does not download the lesson media, process donations, receive payment information or receive donation status.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you voluntarily include. We use it only to respond, troubleshoot, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations—not for advertising. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes. You may request deletion at koushikp@alum.iisc.ac.in unless retention is legally required. Do not send private lesson content unless it is needed and you choose to do so.

Storage, backups and deletion

Version 1.0 has no developer-operated cloud sync. Delete individual items with their in-app controls. Deleting the app removes its local container from the device. Device or iCloud backups may retain an older copy according to your Apple backup settings and Apple’s retention rules.

Children

Raag Riyaz is a general-audience music practice tool and is not designated as a Kids Category app. The app has no account or developer-operated data-collection backend. A parent, guardian or teacher should supervise a child’s use of support email, device sharing and external links.

Security and changes

Your content benefits from the security controls of iOS or iPadOS and the app’s private container, but no storage method is risk-free. We may update this policy when app behavior changes. The effective date above will change, and material changes will be disclosed through the app or its store listing when appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Koushik PG at koushikp@alum.iisc.ac.in.
